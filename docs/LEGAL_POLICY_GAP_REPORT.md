# Heavyar legal and operational gap report

Reviewed: **2026-10-04**. This report lists actual gaps; public policy wording does not pretend they are implemented.

## Priority gaps

1. **Qualified Saudi legal review:** The expanded Arabic-primary suite needs final review by qualified Saudi counsel, including marketplace contracting, consumer cancellation, liability and provider/driver role treatment.
2. **Refund execution:** Backend capability records refunds but production execution remains disabled/not ready. Define authority, approval, Tap/bank execution, reconciliation, status communication and service levels before claiming automated refunds.
3. **Provider settlement:** Tap Marketplace/Split is not verified or enabled. Define the lawful payout model, reconciliation, KYC/compliance needs and evidence of settlement before exposing payout promises.
4. **Retention schedule:** No approved end-to-end category schedule exists for accounts, profiles, listings, requests, chat, ratings, complaints, device tokens, payment records, invoices, audits, backups and security logs. Existing specific values do not close the full gap.
5. **Cross-border transfer governance:** Document data flows by processor and category, destination/access location, necessity, adequacy or safeguards, transfer-risk assessment and processor agreements.

## Marketplace operations gaps

6. **Insurance and accident workflow:** Define evidence, emergency escalation, insurer contacts, ownership, response levels and closure for injury, property damage, theft and traffic events. Heavyar does not currently provide an insurance or damage-waiver product.
7. **Inspection and condition evidence:** Standardize handover/return inspection fields, timestamps, photos, meter/usage evidence, defect notices and retention.
8. **Driver credential coverage:** Driver qualification requirements exist in policy, but live credential verification coverage and expiration monitoring are not complete.
9. **Regulated-category catalogue:** Maintain a counsel-reviewed matrix of licence, permit, operating-card and insurance requirements by equipment/service category. Avoid applying the truck branch to unrelated equipment.
10. **Complaint and appeal service levels:** Define response, evidence, restriction-review, escalation and closure targets. Current policy deliberately promises no unsupported deadline or outcome.
11. **Minors/capacity controls:** The policy states the service is not directed to children, but a complete age/capacity gate and guardian workflow is not proven.

## Finance and tax gaps

12. **VAT/accounting confirmation:** Obtain accounting review of commission/VAT treatment, invoice responsibility and whether/how ZATCA e-invoicing requirements apply. Current public wording does not claim full FATOORA compliance.
13. **Future fee governance:** Create a documented approval and notice process before changing 10%. The 20% proposal remains draft and inactive; historical snapshots must remain immutable.

## Privacy and policy operations gaps

14. **Privacy-rights runbook:** Formalize request intake, identity verification, search/export, exceptions, response tracking and destruction evidence across processors.
15. **Controller identity detail:** Confirm the complete legal controller/trader name and postal/contact particulars counsel requires beyond the published support email and existing business certificate presentation.
16. **Consent/version evidence:** Confirm whether acceptance/version evidence for every material legal-policy update is required and implement it before relying on click acceptance for new obligations.
17. **Translation/legal equivalence:** Arabic is primary and English is drafted as an equivalent, but professional bilingual legal review remains outstanding.
18. **Verification roadmap:** Nafath, government-ID collection, bank/IBAN collection and any expanded verification remain disabled. Any launch requires a new privacy, security, retention and disclosure review before activation.

## Release discipline

- Re-run privacy consistency against mobile source and the Apple privacy declaration for every store release.
- Do not activate 20%, refund automation, Split/payout, Nafath, government-ID or bank-data collection by changing policy text alone.
- Revisit these documents whenever product behavior, processor inventory, payment mode or regulated categories change.
