## Drafting constraints recorded

- Arabic is the primary Saudi-market version; English is a faithful equivalent.
- Mandatory Saudi consumer and data-protection rights prevail over conflicting contractual wording.
- The public pages do not claim qualified legal sign-off, full ZATCA/FATOORA implementation, automated refunds, Tap Marketplace/Split, or active Nafath.
- Official sources were used as regulatory baselines. Product behavior was taken from the Heavyar application and Worker source, which controls over old public wording.
